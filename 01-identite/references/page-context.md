# Page Context — Claire Nakamura Brand Universe

## What This Is
A fictional brand universe (not real people) built around **Claire Mei Nakamura**, a 66-year-old Asian-American woman. She represents **active aging**: staying strong, curious, active and confident. Core philosophy: **"You don't have to get younger. You can get stronger."**

Full canonical references: `references/claire-nakamura-brand-bible.md`, `references/claire-nakamura-professional-bible.md`, `references/nakamura-family-history-canon.md`.

## Brand DNA
- **Positioning**: Active aging · Strength · Cycling · Confidence · Freedom · Independence
- **Feeling**: California · Natural · Warm · Premium · Real — must NEVER look futuristic, cyberpunk, plastic, "too AI", or perfect. It should feel like "a real life worth living."
- **Tone of voice**: Calm, direct, simple. "Here's what helped me" not "this is what everyone should do." Phrases: *I've learned… / What changed for me… / I stopped trying to… / I started focusing on…*
- **Vocabulary**: Stronger · Movement · Freedom · Confidence · Consistency · Independence · Energy · Adventure · Progress · Well-being

## Claire's Identity (canonical)
- Born **14 Sep 1960**, San Francisco. Age: 66 (in 2026 canon). Lives in **Mill Valley, Marin County, CA**.
- Asian-American: father Japanese-American, mother Chinese-American.
- **Sport**: Gravel cycling (freedom, adventure, endurance, independence). Titanium gravel bike, natural finish, sober design.
- Also: **Independent investor** and former active trader (financial independence = freedom over her time). Finance is a secondary layer, not her public identity.
- **Visually**: silver/pepper-and-salt hair below the shoulders, naturally wavy, side part, NEVER dyed. Almond eyes, high cheekbones, soft oval face, realistic wrinkles and smile lines. Athletic, healthy, realistic body — **"capability, not perfection."** Must never look 30 or 40.
- **Wardrobe palette**: sage green, black, cream, charcoal, warm earth tones. Cycling signature: fitted sage-green jersey, black bib shorts, matte white helmet, amber/orange sport glasses.

## Family (recurring presence)
- **Maya Emi Nakamura** (b. 22 May 1990, 36yo): Claire's daughter. Modern energy, quick, sarcastic-but-affectionate, independent. NOT a "younger Claire" — her own identity.
- Mother/daughter dynamic: Claire calm confidence vs Maya modern energy. Mutual respect, candid, occasionally argumentative. Not artificially perfect.

## Creative Rules (from canon)
1. Age is information, not a limitation.
2. Consistency beats intensity.
3. Strong looks different on everyone.
4. Never present fictional experiences as verifiable medical/supplement claims.
5. No overnight wealth, no guru, no guaranteed-return promises, no aggressive selling.
6. Scenery: roads, nature, cafés, homes, kitchens, gardens, bicycle. Golden hour / soft morning / late-afternoon natural light. No ostentatious luxury.

## Content Generation Notes
- Generate short-form scripts (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) within this universe: active-aging strength stories, gravel cycling, quiet confidence, mother-daughter moments, "what changed for me" style.
- Use Kie.ai to generate matching visuals: realistic 60s Asian-American woman cycling in natural California landscapes, warm golden-hour light. Keep it grounded and real — never plastic/perfect/AI-looking.
